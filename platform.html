v3.0
This page manages one platform's acquisition devices. Open it as platform.html?platform=<slug> (e.g. ?platform=bioimaging). Edits stay on this machine until you Publish a new platform.json into that platform's GitLab repo; every lab then sees the devices on next launch. If no ?platform=… is given, nothing loads.
